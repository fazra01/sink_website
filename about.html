About Us

The Sink Cafe is an entirely student-run operation located at 44 Professors Row, Medford, MA, United States, 02155, sponsored by the Office for Campus Life. We rely on our own management and funding to provide a space for community, fun, and (of course) caffeine. Initially founded in a dorm basement as “The Rez,” we now operate in the second floor of the Mayer Campus Center in the space we lovingly call “The Sink.” We collaborate with other student-run clubs, and offer a variety of classic and specialty coffee and tea drinks seven days a week. Come by and see us today!

FAQ

We operate in the second floor of the Mayer Campus Center in Tufts University Medford Campus.

We hire staff to fill available slots at the beginning of each semester based on staffing needs. Typically, we hire about 10 baristas each fall and 1-3 in the Spring. Please be on the lookout for information from this website, our instagram account, or our cafe space in the Mayer Campus Center.

Typically, baristas work anywhere from 2-6 hours per week.

We try to be very accommodating with semester hours and the spontaneity of college life! Our shifts are typically 2-3 hours each, and can be easily dropped and swapped depending on your schedule.

We do! If you qualify for work study through Tufts, you will be able to claim your reward through hours worked at the Sink Cafe.

We have a form for club collaborations that you can fill out! You can also email management at tuftssink@gmail.com with any questions or concerns. Please don’t hesitate to reach out; we’ll respond at our earliest convenience.

We offer gift cards through the Tufts University Marketplace website. Once you purchase a giftcard, the Office for Campus Life will email you with information regarding pickup. You can purchase them here.
